What We Use

Professional-Grade, Start to Finish

Nothing off-the-shelf. Every product used on your vehicle is an industry-standard line chosen for the job — separated by interior and exterior, and matched to your paint, leather, and trim.

Interior

What Goes Inside the Cabin

One reliable all-purpose cleaner does most of the work — the rest are specialists, brought in only when a surface calls for it.

01

P&S

All Star

Our go-to all-purpose interior cleaner — safe and effective across leather, plastics, and painted trim, so most of a cabin gets cleaned with one dependable product.

02

House Mix

Distilled Water & Isopropyl Alcohol

A gentle, streak-free solution reserved for chrome, piano black trim, and touchscreens — safe on the most delicate high-gloss interior surfaces.

03

Koch-Chemie

Leather Star

A leather cleaner and conditioner used for long-term UV protection — lifts dirt and oils from seats and trim while conditioning against cracking and fading.

04

Koch-Chemie

Green Star

An all-purpose cleaner kept in reserve for tougher interior cases — dependable when a surface needs more than the everyday routine.

05

P&S

Carpet Bomber

A heavy-duty carpet & upholstery cleaner brought in for heavy staining — breaks down deep-set dirt before extraction.

Exterior

The Process, Start to Finish

Every exterior detail follows the same order — wheels first, correction only when it's part of the service.

01

Wheels First

Presoak with Green Star, agitated by hand with a wheel brush and face brush. Gentle Snow Foam and Iron Remover come in as needed for stubborn buildup.

02

Prewash & Rinse

Green Star applied to the paint and fully rinsed, clearing surface contamination before any hand contact touches the vehicle.

03

Contact Wash

A Gentle Snow Foam hand wash, followed by Iron Remover on any embedded contamination still on the paint.

04

Decontamination

A clay bar or clay mitt treatment when it's part of the service, lifting anything left bonded to the paint after the wash.

05

Correction — When Included

Meguiar's Ultra Cut Compound and Ultra Finishing Polish, worked with an orbital polisher for vehicles receiving paint correction.

06

Seal & Protect

Finished with Hydro Foam Seal for a durable, hydrophobic layer of protection and gloss.

02
