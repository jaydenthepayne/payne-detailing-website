About Us

Detailing, Built From the Ground Up

The story behind Payne Detailing Group — and the person behind it.

The Founder

Meet Jayden Payne

I started my first business — a landscaping company — in the eighth grade, and grew it to a nine-person crew before I could drive. Since then I've chased a few different ideas, including a run at launching a clothing brand. But cars have always been the constant. Payne Detailing Group is where that passion turned into a real business: full-service, professional-grade detailing built specifically for exotic and luxury vehicle owners who expect the same precision from their detailer that they expect from their car.

I'm 17, a recent graduate, and heading to the Golisano Institute for Business this fall — but this isn't a side project. Every appointment gets the same standard: full attention, professional-grade chemicals, and work done entirely by hand.

— Jayden Payne, Founder
